## Video Specifications
- **Duration**: 15 Seconds (First 2s hook for retention, fast-cut terminal dopamine in the middle, crisp high-value CTA at end)

- **Visual Contrast**: Relaxing Digital Nomad lifestyle (Beach / Cafe, bright aesthetic) vs. Lightning-fast dark-mode terminal automation
